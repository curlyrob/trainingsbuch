What the app does

  • Log workouts with exercises, weight and reps – your sets from last time are already filled in.
  • Save templates such as “Push”, “Pull” or “Legs” and start them with one tap.
  • Check off sets to start a rest timer that alerts you even in the background.
  • Spot new personal records while you train – with a trophy and a little celebration.
  • See your progress per exercise in a chart, plus statistics with weekly history, streaks and sets per muscle group.
  • 75+ exercises by muscle group – add your own in seconds.
  • Note machine settings such as seat height or backrest per exercise – they show up right below the exercise during your workout.
  • Progression hints when you nailed every set last time, plus a plate calculator for the barbell.
  • Your rest runs as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Weights in lb or kg, bodyweight exercises (e.g. pull-ups) and timed exercises (e.g. plank).

Available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian and Finnish.

FAQ

The rest timer doesn’t alert me when the app is in the background.

The app needs permission to send notifications. Check Settings › Notifications › Trainingsbuch. A Focus such as Do Not Disturb can also silence the alert.

How is a “new record” determined?

A set counts as a record if you lift more weight on an exercise than ever before, or if its estimated one-rep max (1RM, Epley formula) is higher than before. The very first time you do an exercise there’s nothing to beat yet, so records start from the second session.

How do I create a template?

Tap the clipboard icon at the top left of the workout list, then “+”. Or save a finished workout via the “⋯” menu with “Save as template”. Weights and reps are always taken from your last workout when you start.

Where do I enter machine settings?

Right in your workout: below each exercise there’s “Add machine settings” – tap it and enter e.g. “seat 4, backrest 2”. You can also change them under Exercises › exercise › Edit.

How do I switch between lb and kg?

Under ⓘ › Settings › Weight unit. Your entries stay exact – only the display changes. That’s also where you set the bar weight for the plate calculator.

How do progression hints work?

If you checked off every set of an exercise last time and matched the reps from the session before, the app suggests adding a little weight today (or one more rep, or 5 seconds). Tap “Apply” to use it. You can turn hints off under ⓘ › Settings.

An exercise is missing.

Just add it yourself: tap “+” on the Exercises tab, or type the name into the search field when picking an exercise and choose “Create … as a new exercise”.

Can I enter decimals?

Yes, e.g. 62.5 kg. Both comma and period work.

Where are my workouts stored?

Only on your iPhone. There’s no account and no server. Your data is included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer).

What does the tip do?

Under ⓘ › Support the developer you can leave an optional tip. It doesn’t unlock anything – all features are free. It simply helps me keep developing the app. Thank you! ❤️

How do I delete data?

Delete workouts, exercises, sets and templates by swiping left. Deleting the app removes all data.
