What the app does

  • Log workouts with exercises, weight and reps – your sets from last time are already filled in.
  • Save templates such as “Push”, “Pull” or “Legs” and start them with one tap.
  • Check off sets to start a rest timer that alerts you even in the background.
  • Spot new personal records while you train – with a trophy and a little celebration.
  • See your progress per exercise in a chart, plus statistics with weekly history, streaks and sets per muscle group.

The app is currently available in German.

FAQ

The rest timer doesn’t alert me when the app is in the background.

The app needs permission to send notifications. Check Settings › Notifications › Trainingsbuch. A Focus such as Do Not Disturb can also silence the alert.

How is a “new record” determined?

A set counts as a record if you lift more weight on an exercise than ever before, or if its estimated one-rep max (1RM, Epley formula) is higher than before. The very first time you do an exercise there’s nothing to beat yet, so records start from the second session.

How do I create a template?

Tap the clipboard icon at the top left of the workout list, then “+”. Or save a finished workout via the “⋯” menu with “Als Vorlage sichern” (save as template). Weights and reps are always taken from your last workout when you start.

Can I enter decimals?

Yes, e.g. 62.5 kg. Both comma and period work.

Where are my workouts stored?

Only on your iPhone. There’s no account and no server. Your data is included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer).

How do I delete data?

Delete workouts, exercises, sets and templates by swiping left. Deleting the app removes all data.
