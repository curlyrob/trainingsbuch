Privacy Policy

for the iPhone app “Trainingsbuch”

Support Privacy Legal notice Deutsch

In short

The app does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone – unless you turn on iCloud sync with Trainingsbuch Pro. Your data is then also stored in your own private iCloud storage at Apple. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. As the developer, I receive no data from the app.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores locally on your iPhone:

  • your workouts with date, optional title and notes,
  • exercises with muscle group, and the weight and reps of every set,
  • your templates, machine settings for exercises and settings such as the rest duration,
  • optionally: your height and body weight, if you enter them.

Statistics, charts and records are calculated on your iPhone from these entries. The data is not transferred to me or any third party and is not shared with Apple Health. It is part of your regular iPhone backup, managed by Apple or your computer according to your own settings.

iCloud sync (Trainingsbuch Pro)

With Trainingsbuch Pro you can turn on iCloud sync (Info › Trainingsbuch Pro › iCloud Sync). Your workouts, exercises, templates and body weight history are then stored via Apple’s CloudKit service in the private database of your iCloud account and synced with your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account.

This data lives only in your iCloud storage. As the developer, I have no access to it and can neither view nor analyse it. Apple is responsible for storing it in iCloud; Apple’s privacy policy applies. Your height and settings stay on each device even with sync turned on.

Without Pro, sync is off. After purchase it is turned on and can be turned off again at any time; your data then stays on the device. You can delete the data stored in iCloud under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Trainingsbuch.

Permissions

Notifications

When you first start the rest timer, the app asks whether it may send you notifications. It uses them to let you know when your rest is over, even while the app is in the background. These notifications are scheduled directly on your iPhone – nothing is sent over the internet.

The app uses no other permissions (such as camera, photos, contacts or location). You can change the notification permission at any time in your iPhone’s Settings › Notifications.

In-app purchases (Pro and tips)

You can buy Trainingsbuch Pro or voluntarily leave a tip in the app. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I don’t receive any payment or personal data from you – Apple only provides me with anonymous sales figures. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Deletion

You can delete workouts, exercises and templates in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all data stored in it. If you used iCloud sync, delete the copy in iCloud as described under “iCloud sync” above.

Your rights

As I do not process any data about you, there is no data I could provide information about, correct or delete. You are still welcome to contact me by email with any privacy questions. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page. GitHub’s privacy policy applies. The site itself uses no cookies, no tracking and loads no content from other providers. The “Download on the App Store” link leads to Apple.

Changes

If data processing changes – for example due to new features – this policy will be updated.
